## Spherometer

The three rigid outer legs lie on an equilateral triangle of radius r. The top view shows all contact points and l = √3 r. Perspective rotates the viewing direction so the rear leg is separated from the central screw. Glass vertices follow the same spherical cap as the contact model. The section shows the outer-tip plane, central contact and vertical signed height. The optional vertical exaggeration is explicitly labeled ×12; numerical dimensions are unchanged.

The screw advances 0.50 mm per revolution, with 50 divisions and a 0.01 mm least count. It cannot move below the first nonpenetrating division at the selected surface. Record a plane contact, then a convex or concave contact: h is the recorded reading difference, not the configured hidden sagitta. R = (r²+h²)/(2h) = l²/(6h)+h/2. Convex is positive and concave negative. h = 0 gives an infinite radius, not a made-up finite number. Quantization can leave a gap smaller than one division; force, elastic tip deformation and contact pressure are not modeled. Radius uncertainty is not presented as a direct screw least count.

Current reading, reference, signed h and measured R remain together in the compact workspace. Reveal/Check opens the simulated signed radius for comparison. Numeric alternatives, actual knob dragging, magnified circular scale, Perspective/Top/Section, camera pan/zoom/fit and full-screen use one Experiment instance. View changes do not modify measurements. Old direct-reading sessions and trial bodies remain supported; their estimate is retained when opened in the new workflow. The current clean layout has no development label, permanent section navigation or restoration snackbar. Large text scrolls only the bounded dock when necessary.

## Thermal materials and calculations

The registry contains all 36 requested names with grade/condition descriptions, property availability, units, reference temperature, source URL, directionality and procedural appearance. **16 records support solid conduction; 13 support heating. These are not 36 fully qualified material-property datasets.** Missing scalar properties and unmatched grades are deliberately unavailable; ranges are retained as ranges, not silently converted to nominal constants. Unsupported experiments cannot record a calculated result. The exact per-property limitations are listed in THERMAL_MATERIALS.json.

Conduction compares two or three equal-geometry solid samples with common boundary temperatures and area, calling the existing conduction function. The temperature profile is the exact steady 1D linear solution, not a transient simulation. Heating compares equal mass or equal volume under equal heater power: Q = Pt = mcΔT, zero loss, constant cp, well-mixed samples, no phase change. Composite layers use L/(kA) plus an explicit total K/W contact resistance per interface; heat flow is common and interface temperature drops are retained. Fluids cannot enter the solid-rod solver. No internal convection, melting, boiling or steam is claimed.

Property temperature dependence is not invented. Calculations impose a narrow ±10 K teaching window around each record's reference temperature; this is an approximation guard, not a sourced empirical validity range. BOROFLOAT explicitly retains k at 90 °C, cp over 20–100 °C and density at 25 °C. Records at incompatible temperatures cannot be freely compared. Generic ranges and unqualified grades remain available for inspection with calculations disabled.

The natural appearance comes from the selected record's color/texture descriptor. It does not set conductivity or heat capacity. Copper, brushed aluminium/stainless/brass, cast surfaces, glass, ceramics, grains/veins, wood direction, pores, fibres and transparent liquids use original procedural drawing. The optional thermal overlay maps calculated temperatures to a labeled false-color legend; ordinary warm copper does not emit red light. These are material-shaded educational drawings, not photographs.

Primary sources consulted include [ANSYS Motor-CAD material help](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/MotorCAD/v252/en/Motor-CAD_UG/MotorCAD/topics/materialhelp.html), [ASHRAE building material properties](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F25/SI/F25_Ch26/F25_Ch26_si.aspx), [Copper Development Association C51000](https://alloys.copper.org/alloy/C51000), [SCHOTT BOROFLOAT technical details](https://www.schott.com/en-gb/products/b/borofloat/content/technical-details), [Crystran fused silica](https://www.crystran.com/optical-materials/fused-silica-sio2/), [CoorsTek AD-995 data](https://www.coorstek.com/media/4235/advanced-alumina.pdf), and [ROCKWOOL RWA45](https://www.rockwool.com/uk/products/rwa45/). Each registry record identifies the particular source and any missing conditions. Generic NIST/source portals on unavailable records are not citations for invented property values.

## Optical library and ray tracing

One registry supplies all 40 requested component names and eight optical materials. The existing lab's purpose remains clear through its default apparatus. Geometry, optical material, position, rotation, scale, supported custom-prism apex, wavelength, source direction/elevation and illustrative coating reflectance are saved. Components can be added, selected, moved, duplicated and removed; numeric alternatives and optional snapping are available. Single rays, parallel bundles and a three-wavelength visible sample are supported. Normals, incidence angles and branch power can be shown. Focus selected, fit all and the shared camera controls affect the view only.

The tracer intersects finite planar and quadratic boundaries analytically, tracks the medium on each side, applies vector Snell refraction, reflection, TIR and lossless unpolarized Fresnel power, and traces internal and exiting branches. Sphere, cylindrical, elliptical and parabolic normals come from the actual implicit surface gradients. Epsilon offsets avoid repeated self-hits; depth, ray-count and minimum-power caps account for terminated power. Results distinguish escaped, absorbed and bounded-tail power. Bulk absorption is unavailable and is not inferred from tint. The splitter's user-selected reflectance is an illustrative prescribed lossless coating, not a fabricated commercial specification; other designated ideal mirror faces assume R = 1.

SCHOTT [N-BK7](https://media.schott.com/api/public/content/41e799d0bf874807a0bb8e702fbb75b5?v=54856406), [N-F2](https://media.schott.com/api/public/content/061f3156c83a44ed9220770b0f65a869?v=d69b35e0) and [Malitson fused silica](https://opg.optica.org/josa/abstract.cfm?uri=josa-55-10-1205) use sourced Sellmeier coefficients over the visible subset. Water, acrylic, polycarbonate and glycerol use explicitly monochromatic reference approximations; wavelength/spectrum controls reject unsupported use. Air is explicitly ideal n = 1 rather than a humidity/pressure calculation. Polymer teaching values cite [Edmund Optics](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/optics/understanding-optical-windows/); reagent glycerol cites [ITW](https://www.itwreagents.com/download_file/tds/131339/en/tds_131339_en.pdf). Full conditions are in OPTICAL_MATERIALS.json.

The corner cube uses a genuine 3D tetrahedral volume and three orthogonal ideal reflecting faces; the viewport is explicitly an XY projection and reports exit xyz. Roof, Porro and Dove entries are labeled valid sections, without claiming a complete Porro pair or image inversion/rotation. Cylindrical lenses use the powered meridional section and do not focus along the extrusion axis. Mirrors and lens shapes have finite-aperture analytic surfaces. Liquid cells/prisms have nested glass walls and full liquid fill; no meniscus or fluid dynamics. The 40-entry functional evidence is for these stated scopes, not certification of full commercial optical assemblies. Separate placed-component footprints must not overlap; built-in nested liquid walls are supported.

Twelve guided presets cover slab displacement, semicircular radial entry and critical angle, prism incidence and minimum deviation, wedge deviation, TIR, ghost rays, splitter power, liquid index, lens convergence and 3D retroreflection. All rays are computed from their configured boundaries. The prism minimum-deviation explanation states the air/prism geometry and symmetric-path assumption; no single-ray image-rotation claim is made. Colored paths are labeled educational overlays rather than visible beams photographed in clear air.

Records capture actual calculated readings and the complete source/configuration versions. Optical main readings use the dominant exit's angular deviation where defined, with detailed branch observations and power accounting retained in the trial. The existing CSV exporter includes configuration JSON and guards spreadsheet cells. Labeled workspace PNG export uses the existing share plugin and opens a share picker only on learner action; QA captures bytes without selecting a recipient.
