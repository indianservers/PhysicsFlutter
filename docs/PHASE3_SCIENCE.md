# Phase 3 scientific implementation

Model identifier: `energy-gravity-oscillation-3.0`. Stable new lab IDs: s05-e00 through s07-e05. All stored observations are computed SI values. Slider reference units (Earth radii, Earth/Moon masses, km/s, degrees) are converted explicitly. Numerical displays round only the presentation.

## Shared clock, coordinates and limits

The existing wall-clock accumulator drives 120 fixed ticks per display second; at most twelve ticks / 0.1 simulated display-seconds are accepted per render callback. Excess catch-up is dropped rather than producing a large integration jump. At selector 1×, local experiments advance in physical seconds; orbit, escape and Kepler ticks each represent four physical seconds (480× time), labeled in the workspace. Speed selectors multiply the clock request. Navigation, backgrounding, editing and dialogs pause; restored sessions remain paused. The 120-display-second observation horizon is not evidence of physical escape or damping equilibrium.

+x is right and +y up. Canvas inversion is confined to rendering. Pendulum angle is measured from downward vertical toward +x, stored in radians and displayed in degrees. Horizontal oscillators use displacement about equilibrium, rather than total spring length. World zoom and pan do not modify model coordinates. Gesture inverses use the painter's current view scale; precise validated numeric alternatives are provided.

Graphs use at most 301 actual trajectory samples, normally every eight physics ticks plus explicit steps/endpoints. Parameter curves are independently labeled analytic evaluations, never exported as recorded trials. A cursor selects an existing sample. In the static supported-body investigations time remains zero; no pretend orbital motion is added to a field measurement.

## Work, Energy & Power

1. **Work (s05-e00).** Constant force at angle θ, horizontal Coulomb contact. N=mg−Fsinθ; parameter changes limit upward force to keep N≥0. The supported model uses equal static and kinetic coefficients μ, with static friction supplying only the required value up to μN. Exact constant-acceleration/zero-speed segments reuse Phase 2 friction events. WF=Fcosθ Δx, Wg=WN=0. Friction loss is accumulated from applied work minus ΔK on each exact segment, with small negative roundoff suppressed. Wnet=WF−loss=ΔK. Travel includes a within-tick reversal; it is distinct from signed displacement. The selected travel boundary halts observation with one-tick resolution, without imposing an unmodeled braking impulse. Controls: m 0.2–5 kg, F 0–40 N, θ ±180°, travel 0.2–10 m, μ 0–0.8, initial speed ±3 m/s. No varying-force model is offered.
2. **Kinetic Energy (s05-e01).** Translational K=mv²/2, m 0.2–5 kg, signed v ±10 m/s. Cart translation follows x=vt; the camera follows it. Wheel bearings are illustrative and rotational inertia is neglected. The lesson explicitly contrasts total rolling energy Ktrans+Iω²/2. The K-versus-speed curve is analytic, not measured.
3. **Potential Energy (s05-e02).** Prescribed quasi-static four-second lift in uniform g, with current U=mg(h−hzero), ΔU=mgh and full target ΔU=mgH. Changing hzero shifts U, not the lift difference. Controls: height 0–10 m, reference ±5 m, g 1–20 m/s² and mass 0.2–5 kg. This position demonstration excludes actuator acceleration/kinetic energy; it does not substitute mgh at orbital distances.
4. **Energy Conservation (s05-e03).** Bilateral captive bead on y=x²/(2R), eliminating any assumption that an unsupported cart can stay on an open loop. With q=x, K=m(1+q²/R²)q̇²/2, U=mgq²/(2R), and generalized viscous force −c q̇, the equation is q̈=−[q q̇²/R²+gq/R+(c/m)q̇]/(1+q²/R²). RK4 advances q,q̇,Eloss with Ėloss=c q̇². K+U+loss is the budget; both mechanical and full budget are displayed. R 1–4 m, q0 ±2.5 m, q̇0 ±2 m/s, c 0–3 kg/s. Unequal scene-axis scales are labeled. No rotational bead energy, solid-height or unilateral contact model is claimed.
5. **Power (s05-e04).** Ideal constant-speed lift segment: v=H/duration, F=mg, W(t)=mgh(t), Pavg=mgH/duration and Pinst=mgv during motion, zero after the segment. End transients are excluded, stated explicitly. Load 0.2–30 kg, height 0–8 m, duration 0.5–20 s. Drum/sheave rotation and rope position follow the lift distance.
6. **Efficiency (s05-e05).** Specified η∈[0,1], not an inferred real motor property. A five-second constant-input-power segment transfers Euseful=ηEin and Eloss=(1−η)Ein; load rise=Euseful/(mg). Input parameter 0–1000 J. Zero input gives zero useful/loss energy and explicitly undefined measured output/input ratio, while η remains the specified model parameter. No internal energy storage or motor transient is implied.

## Gravitation

G=6.67430×10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻² (NIST). Earth reference μ=3.98600435507×10¹⁴ m³/s² (JPL), Mreference=μ/G, spherical reference radius 6,371,000 m, Moon mass reference 7.342×10²² kg. The model source radius stays one reference Earth radius when mass is changed, so density changes. This is not an ephemeris or an oblate-Earth geodesy model. Source mass sliders span 0.1–3 Earth reference masses.

7. **Universal Gravitation (s06-e00).** F=Gm1m2/r², force vectors equal and opposite. Supported fixed bodies, not a two-body evolution. Companion mass 0.1–3 Moon reference masses; companion physical radius assumed 0.273 Earth radii. Centre separation 3–80 Earth radii guarantees non-overlap. Drawn companion size is exaggerated for visibility.
8. **Gravitational Field (s06-e01).** Exterior vector g=−μr/r³, independent of test mass. Radius 1.01–12 Earth radii and polar angle ±180°. Exterior radial grid arrows indicate direction; the numerical field is authoritative. No interior model.
9. **Gravitational Potential (s06-e02).** V=−μ/r (J/kg), U=mV (J), zero at infinity. Same exterior radius/angle controls, probe mass 1–2000 kg. Equipotential circles are overlays; no field interior or motion is implied.
10. **Orbits (s06-e03).** Fixed spherical source/test satellite. Initial r 1.05–5 Earth radii, vr ±4 km/s, vt ±16 km/s. Velocity Verlet advances position and velocity with the actual inverse-square acceleration, four physical seconds/tick. Tracks are not forced circular. ε=v²/2−μ/r, hz=r×v, e=√(1+2εhz²/μ²), rp=hz²/[μ(1+e)]. For ε<0 and rp>R only, a=−μ/(2ε), period=2π√(a³/μ) and ra=a(1+e) are reported. A surface-crossing tick is bisected forty times using the same partial-step propagation and then stopped. State retains incident velocity; no bounce or post-impact trajectory is modeled. The tested supported step is small compared with a surface traversal; no satellite passes through the planet. Fixed source means satellite back-reaction is excluded.
11. **Escape Velocity (s06-e04).** Same propagation with vr −4…16 km/s, vt ±10 km/s, r 1.05–5 Earth radii. Escape reference √(2μ/r); energy classification and collision are separate. The UI labels an approximately zero-energy boundary within 1 J/kg to avoid interpreting rounded values as a sign change. Screen-edge passage never defines escape. No atmosphere, thrust, rotation or other bodies. Presets are bounded by the supported slider ranges; the displayed component value shows any range limitation.
12. **Kepler’s Laws (s06-e05).** Exact planar ellipse about a fixed focus, a 1.2–12 Earth radii, e 0–0.8 with a(1−e)>R. Invalid UI combinations raise a to keep periapsis exterior. Mean anomaly nt, n=√(μ/a³); solve M=E−esinE with sixteen Newton iterations. Position a(cosE−e), a√(1−e²)sinE; analytic velocity uses dE/dt=n/(1−ecosE). Equal-duration sectors each span T/12 at two different locations, with analytic area hΔt/2. T²=4π²a³/μ. Circular e=0 is supported continuously. This studio uses Earth-scale satellites rather than silently mixing solar μ with Earth masses. Scene body sizes/scene time scale are labeled.

## Oscillations

All spring models are horizontal: gravity is balanced by the rail and no vertical-equilibrium extension is hidden. Core mass 0.2–5 kg, stiffness 5–80 N/m, x0 ±0.25 m and v0 ±0.5 m/s. A finite drawing auto-fits actual displacements without clipping numerical values or changing equations; spring natural lengths, mass widths and perspective are illustrative.

13. **Spring SHM (s07-e00).** Exact x=x0cosω0t+(v0/ω0)sinω0t; v and a differentiated analytically. ω0=√(k/m), T=2π/ω0. Coil endpoints attach to the actual moving mass edge.
14. **Pendulum (s07-e01).** Nonlinear θ̈=−(g/L)sinθ, RK4 with four substeps per 1/120 s tick. Length 0.3–2 m, gravity 1–20 m/s², initial angle ±70°, angular velocity ±2 rad/s additionally restricted by E/(mgL)≤1−cos75°. This guarantees a turning angle at most 75° and positive tension m(gcosθ+Lθ̇²). No slack-string transition is offered. Same-direction zero crossings yield a measured period after two crossings. Predicted nonlinear period 4√(L/g)K(sin(θmax/2)) uses 256-panel Simpson quadrature; 2π√(L/g) is separately labeled the small-angle estimate. At exact zero motion a crossing period is unavailable, not fabricated.
15. **Energy Exchange (s07-e02).** Same exact ideal spring solver, K=mv²/2, U=kx²/2, E=K+U. The lesson explains the 2f energy variation. Bar values and graph traces share the actual state.
16. **Damping (s07-e03).** Exact homogeneous solution of mẍ+bẋ+kx=0 for under/critical/overdamping, b 0–50 kg/s. At |ω0²−(b/2m)²|<10⁻¹⁰ω0² the critical formula is used. Loss=Einitial−E(t). For underdamping the envelope coefficient includes both initial displacement and velocity; the envelope is absent outside that regime. Dashpot geometry is a labeled linear viscous model, not a fluid simulation. Presets outside the supported damping range are clamped visibly.
17. **Resonance (s07-e04).** Exact homogeneous transient plus sinusoidal particular solution for mẍ+bẋ+kx=F0cosωt. F0 0–5 N, ω 0–15 rad/s, b 0–50 kg/s. A=F0/√[(k−mω²)²+(bω)²], phase=atan2(bω,k−mω²). UI distinguishes ω0, real free ωd, and positive displacement-response peak √(ω0²−b²/2m²); zero indicates no positive peak. For b=0 and |ω−ω0|<10⁻⁹ω0, the explicit resonant particular solution F0 t sinω0t/(2mω0) is used; no finite steady amplitude is recorded. Away from resonance with zero damping the homogeneous part persists, so the curve is not a settled measurement. Finite analytic parameter-curve samples exclude undefined singular values; growing trajectory values are never silently capped.
18. **Coupled Oscillators (s07-e05).** Drawn topology wall–k1–m1–kc–m2–k2–wall. Mẍ+Kx=0 with diagonal masses and K=[[k1+kc,−kc],[−kc,k2+kc]]. Diagonalize symmetric M⁻¹/²KM⁻¹/², project both initial positions and velocities into mass-weighted eigenvectors and propagate each eigenmode analytically. Independent m1,m2, k1,k2, kc 0–30 N/m and both initial x/v controls. Equal-mass presets demonstrate in/out-of-phase modes; a one-mass release with weak coupling shows beats. Total U=k1x1²/2+k2x2²/2+kc(x2−x1)²/2. Local energy assigns half the coupling energy to each body, explicitly documented. The energy bound sets a common drawing scale so mass icons and springs remain separate.
