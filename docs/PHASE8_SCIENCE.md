## Shared conversions, coordinates and clocks

AU=149597870700 m exactly. Parsec=AU×648000/π; light-year=c×365.25×86400 s. Nominal solar GM=1.3271244e20 m³/s², radius=6.957e8 m, luminosity=3.828e26 W. Solar mass in kg is nominal GM divided by the existing G=6.67430e−11 m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻², and inherits G uncertainty; it is not an exact measured solar mass. Source: [IAU2015 Resolution B3](https://www.iau.org/static/resolutions/IAU2015_English.pdf). c, h, kB, σ and the reduced-mass hydrogen Rydberg model reuse the existing Waves, Thermal and Modern Physics layers rather than conflicting copies. The thermal σ constant is used consistently when deriving stellar radius.

Data are double precision. Position, speed, luminosity, distance, energy and physical time are SI internally; controls explicitly convert days, AU, kpc and nominal solar units. A trial’s primary quantity/unit is SI; serialized control values retain their declared input units. Derived-state units, graph-axis convention, seed, model/dataset version, notes and initial/current controls are retained. Each model labels its epoch convention: J2000 TDB for orbits, model stellar age for MIST, vacuum rest frame for spectra, static catalog/potential for galaxies, a0=1 convention for FLRW, inferior conjunction for unnamed transit systems.

The shared120 Hz FixedClock is reused. Orbits advance one Julian year per playback second before speed adjustment. Stellar playback20 s corresponds to the full tabulated age interval; starting later ends earlier. Transit playback20 s sweeps0.08 orbital periods from the selected phase. These are explicit accelerated teaching clocks, not wall dates. Samples’ `t` is playback seconds, separately identified by `observationAxis`; actual age/days-from-J2000/transit seconds are stored in scientific state. Background, hidden sections and pushed routes pause; resume never auto-advances lost time. Histories are capped301 rows and replay uses saved parameters/ticks, not rendering cadence.

## Solar System Orbits — s16-e00

Eight planet elements come from [JPL approximate planetary positions, Table1](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html): a,e,I,L,longitude of perihelion and node at J2000.0. The Earth entry is explicitly the **Earth–Moon barycentre**. Table1 fitting originally covers1800–2050; this app freezes the epoch elements and omits the secular rates. The supported ±200 Julian-year elapsed slider is an educational two-body evolution range, **not** that fitted ephemeris method or an accuracy guarantee over those dates. No calendar-specific high-precision position is advertised.

Mean anomaly M0=(L−ϖ)rad, M=M0+sqrt(μ/a³)t; existing `keplerAt` solves E−e sinE=M. In-plane coordinates a(cosE−e),a√(1−e²)sinE and their analytic velocities are rotated through ω=ϖ−Ω,I,Ω into J2000 ecliptic axes. The scene shows an explicitly projected orbital plane; z is retained in state. Period P=2π√(a³/μ), instantaneous radius/speed and semi-major axis are distinct. Mean path speed is ellipse circumference/P, using a256-interval Simpson complete elliptic integral, cached by eccentricity (bounded32 entries). Sun nominal μ only, massless planets, no mutual perturbations, light time, aberration, precession or Horizons ephemeris.

Physical mode uses a common linear distance scale; compressed mode uses a labeled square-root radius mapping. Both exaggerate body sizes separately, and the large selected globe is an illustrative inset. Neither arrangement is a current measured sky. Orbit outlines are fixed101-point paths cached separately for two scale modes; current markers come from actual propagated state. Native comparison rows include all eight planets’ a,r,P,instantaneous and mean speed. Epoch geometry, circular cases, period repeatability and vis-viva are tested.

## Stellar Life — s16-e01

Actual **MIST1.2 / MESA7503** solar-metallicity, nonrotating EEP tracks at exactly **1,3,15 M☉**. [Official model grid](https://mist.science/model_grids.html), [Choi et al.2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08592), [Dotter2016 EEP method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.05144). Metadata: [Fe/H]=0,[α/Fe]=0,Yinit0.2703,Zinit0.0142857,v/vcrit0. Track names00100M,00300M,01500M; respectively1710,1710,808 rows. Selected columns are physical age in years, log10(L/L☉), log10(Teff/K), phase. No invented evolutionary sequence or interpolation across masses. [Archive/file checksums and metadata](phase8-track-sources.json) identify the actual downloaded source.

Age search is binary; logL/logT interpolate linearly **within the same source phase**. At phase boundaries the preceding point is held until the next phase begins, rather than interpolating incompatible phases. Full source rows are retained. H–R visualization uses negative log10 temperature on x (hot to the left) and log10 luminosity on y; phase boundaries and regular6-row samples bound the graph to roughly300 vertices. Labels show actual K and W separately. R is derived from L=4πR²σTeff⁴ using the stated nominal L☉ conversion; it is not the raw MESA logR column, and tiny source-constant normalization differences are not a metrological radius claim.

Stage-jump buttons select the true reference age. Source phase5 is labeled late giant/advanced evolution, avoiding a universal low-mass TP-AGB interpretation for the high-mass track. The1/3 M☉ models reach hot post-AGB/early remnant evolution; the supported view does not supply a complete white-dwarf cooling history. The15 M☉ track ends before the compact remnant. Broad endpoint descriptions (white dwarf versus core-collapse/compact-remnant outcome) are qualitative and model dependent, not exact universal mass thresholds or computed neutron-star/black-hole masses. Rotation, composition, mass loss and binarity change outcomes. Schematic granulation/color/log-compressed size are original illustrations driven by the track’s L/T/R, not observations or arbitrary images defining the track.

## Spectroscopy — s16-e02

Planck Bλ=2hc²/[λ⁵ expm1(hc/λkT)], with a stable exponential and Wien b=2.897771955e−3 m K **for Bλ**. Bν peaks differently. The displayed continuum is unit-peak normalized; it is not absolute flux at a detector or a full stellar atmosphere. Four illustrative Gaussian absorption deficits use the earlier hydrogen **vacuum** Balmer3…6→2 wavelengths. Their depth series0.50,0.44,0.38,0.32 and intrinsic σ0.12nm are teaching parameters, not abundances, line strengths or a fitted star.

Positive radial velocity is receding; longitudinal relativistic Doppler factor sqrt((1+β)/(1−β)), |v|≤0.02c. No cosmological-redshift interpretation is attached to this local velocity. Gaussian instrumental FWHM=λ/R, σinst=FWHM/2.354820045; broadened σ²=σintrinsic²+σinst² and depth is reduced by σintrinsic/σ, preserving each local deficit area. This treats the continuum as locally smooth rather than convolving an entire atmosphere. R500…10000 changes actual calculated depth/width. A900-interval baseline plus81 samples around each shifted/rest line (spacingσ/8 across±5σ) avoids undersampling narrow features; fewer than1600 profile points. Current and stationary reference spectra are separate curves. The detector’s color strip is an illustrative false-color display, not perceived stellar color. No observation is labeled Vega, temperature/composition is never inferred from arbitrary RGB. Sources: existing Phase7 hydrogen/CODATA documentation and [Planck-law treatment, OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-1-blackbody-radiation).

## Galaxies — s16-e03

Axisymmetric potential at z=0: Hernquist bulge Φb=−GMb/(R+c), c0.6kpc; Miyamoto–Nagai disk Φd=−GMd/√[R²+(a+b)²], b0.3kpc; optional spherical logarithmic halo Φh=vh² ln[(R²+rc²)/rc²]/2, rc4kpc. Circular speed squared is **R∂Φ/∂R**. Components add in v², not v. Thus vb²=GMbR/(R+c)², vd²=GMdR²/[R²+(a+b)²]^(3/2), vh,c²=vh²R²/(R²+rc²). The disk is never treated as an exact spherical enclosed mass. Reported v²R/G is explicitly a *spherical-equivalent* mass, not the disk’s true enclosed mass. The logarithmic halo is an idealized potential over supported radii, not a finite total-mass or escape-speed model. Sources: [Hernquist1990](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1990ApJ...356..359H), [Miyamoto & Nagai1975](https://doi.org/10.1093/pasj/27.4.533).

SPARC **NGC2403** provides73 original radii, observed circular speeds and quoted velocity errors from [Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251). The source file assumes distance3.16Mpc; that is retained. [Original text copy](phase8-NGC2403-source.dat); transport [Zenodo mirror16284118](https://zenodo.org/records/16284118), not a new catalog/evaluation. Source kpc/km/s columns and errors convert centrally to m/m/s; observed points retain uncertainty bars and cursor values. They are separate from the adjustable illustrative model, which is **not a fit** to this galaxy. Morphology is a labeled original schematic with a model-radius probe, not a calibrated image or mass map. Different components are not uniquely inferred from a curve without assumptions.

## Cosmology — s16-e04

Flat FLRW ΛCDM: E²=Ωr(1+z)^4+Ωm(1+z)^3+ΩΛ, ΩΛ=1−Ωm−Ωr. Ωr=0.00009 is fixed and explicit; supported z0…5,H0 50…90km/s/Mpc,Ωm0.1…0.5. This retains radiation over that modest redshift range but does not model arbitrary neutrino masses, curvature, w(z), recombination or precision early-universe physics. User-selected values are hypothetical, not measured parameter estimates.

DC=c/H0∫dz/E; lookback=1/H0∫dz/[(1+z)E]; DL=(1+z)DC; DA=DC/(1+z); a=1/(1+z). Light-travel distance is c×lookback, distinct from DC. Present recession convention vrec,0=H0DC can exceed c and is not local Doppler speed. cz and cz/H0 are used only as tested low-z limits, never exact high-z laws. [Hogg1999](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9905116), [Wright2006 calculator methodology](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0609593).

Composite Simpson1024 even intervals; zero redshift returns exact zero integrals. Tested against independently coded Python two-point Gauss–Legendre10000-panel references and refinement. Reference ratios agree within3e−10 at the included z values; source and results in `tool/phase8_independent_reference.py` / `screenshots/phase8-independent-cosmology.json`. The121-point graph is computed outside widget build and cached for redshift-probe edits. The schematic homogeneous grid has spacing proportional to actual scale factor, fills the viewport at all supported z, and has no preferred origin or Big Bang explosion into space.

## Exoplanets — s16-e05

Unnamed circular system, zero planet mass, opaque planet and **uniform** stellar disk. a=[GMstar(P/2π)²]^(1/3). Coordinates at phaseθ=2πt/P: x=a sinθ,y=a cosi cosθ, foreground iff cosθ>0; t0 is inferior conjunction. b=(a/Rs)cosi. Circle lens overlap uses exact disjoint, containment and acos/lens branches, not Rp²/Rs² during grazing. Flux=1−Aoverlap/(πRs²); middle depth is computed at conjunction. First/fourth-contact duration P/π asin{√[(1+k)²−b²]/[(a/Rs)sini]}, or zero for no transit. All radii/period/mass controls are mutually Kepler-consistent. Exact source geometry drives the scene and light curve; a graph cursor updates the actual phase/scene. The uniform disk is intentionally not decorated with limb darkening. Source: [Mandel & Agol2002](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0210099), uniform-source geometry.

The241-point full curve is a **generated synthetic reference**, not data collected over playback. Optional independent Gaussian noise uses the shared xorshift32/Box–Muller stream and a saved integer seed; flux noise may exceed1 and is never silently clipped. Model flux and noisy samples are distinct. Trials preserve the actual selected calculated state plus generated profile, parameters, model and seed. No real planet name gets arbitrary parameters. Unsupported limb-darkening coefficients, stellar spots, eccentricity, relativistic effects and fitted observed planet data are not offered.

## Comparisons, learning and persistence

[Tool specifications](PHASE8_TOOLS.md) define all five analogies and their failure boundaries. Models reuse `freeOscillator`, `Electrostatic.field`, `opticalIntensity`, `harmonic`, `rcState`, `probeTemperature`, `trackPosition` and `MatterDistribution`. Phase-sweep/radius-scan playback is an explicitly parameterized comparison coordinate, not invented common physical time. First-order normalized time maps each model through its own τ. Deterministic classical motion and conditional detector probabilities retain separate axes/units; quantum events are independent preparations, not trajectories. The matter sampler uses the earlier finite4096-grid CDF and seed, at50 detections per observation second, at most1000 during the20-second comparison.

SQLite remains schema3; extensible versioned JSON needs no destructive rewrite. Astronomy model validation adds to the existing accepted-model list; earlier models remain accepted. Favorites/recent IDs are additive preferences; paths read the same completed-ID set. New model/source/epoch metadata accompany saves. Incompatible astronomy reference/model sessions are held intact and automatic persistence is blocked until explicit adoption saves a visible backup. Preset-default launch also preserves a prior configuration under a backup key, then uses one transient default request; no fabricated observations/completion. Mastery attempts store stable ID, source model/configuration, answer/target/units/tolerance and UTC time. Corrupt attempt bodies are skipped with a recovery notice and retained on disk. No inference of scientifically validated ability is made.

Reproduction and actual measurements are in PHASE8_VALIDATION.md. iOS, physical Android, independent screen-reader users and comprehensive Phase9 science/art review remain unverified until actually performed.
