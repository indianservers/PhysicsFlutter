# Phase 4 science, sampling and privacy

Model `waves-optics-4.0`, independent of Flutter in `lib/core/physics/waves_optics.dart`. The twelve [lab specifications](PHASE4_LAB_SPECIFICATIONS.md) give equations, SI units, assumptions, control ranges, worked examples and stable IDs for every lab. The supplied PNGs are visual references, not scientific evidence.

## Conventions and analytic state

Positive displacement is upward; +x is right. Right-traveling harmonic motion is A sin(2πx/λ − 2πft + φ), left-traveling reverses the temporal sign. Frequency and wavelength are independent teaching-medium inputs and speed is derived fλ. This is a nondispersive height model, not full water dynamics. A fixed probe oscillates locally; the disturbance travels.

Pulses are signed Gaussians A exp(−(x−x₀−sct)²/(2σ²)), s=±1. An infinite linear string is viewed through a finite window; pulses continue after overlap and leave the window without artificial reflection. Equal opposite pulses cancel at coincident centres.

Mechanical Interference sums two equal-frequency circular phase waves at (−d/2,0),(d/2,0). Constant amplitude has no geometric decay or source singularity and is explicitly labeled. With complex phasor C=e^(ikr₁)+q e^(i(kr₂+φ)), displacement is A Im(Ce^(−iωt)), amplitude A|C|, and normalized mean squared-height intensity proxy |C|²/(1+q)². The factor 1/2 in mean squared displacement cancels in normalization. This is neither calibrated acoustic intensity nor the optical far-field fringe approximation. Grid x∈[−1,1] m, z∈[−.5,.5] m; displayed field coordinates/probe use that same mapping. Wavelength is constrained to at least 16/63 m: eight samples per shortest wavelength along the 64-point horizontal grid. Frequency is dependently limited after speed edits. Spatial profiles use 257 points; cached 64×32 phasors are rotated for time updates. Intensity view uses the same phasors without temporal oscillation.

Standing waves are the sum of two A/2 traveling waves, yielding A sin(nπx/L) cos(ωt). v=√(T/μ), λ=2L/n, f=nv/(2L), n=1…6. Fixed ends, n+1 nodes including ends, n antinodes. A≤.02L/n restricts the small-amplitude string model; probe≤L. Displacement profiles use 513 points. No arbitrary driven-string or nonlinear tension dynamics.

Doppler is strictly collinear, stationary observer, constant subsonic source in the medium frame, emitting steadily for all times. Source position x₀+vₛτ at emission time τ satisfies c(t−τ)=|x_obs−x₀−vₛτ|. Both sign branches are checked for causal consistency; frequency uses the emission-side sign and c/(c−vₛ toward observer). No arbitrary 2-D pass-by formula is applied. At source/observer coincidence the received frequency is undefined, rather than a fabricated value. Signed Mach number is limited to ±.7. Latest 32 displayed fronts retain each actual emission position/time and radius c(t−τ). To make fronts legible every ceil(.03f)th crest is drawn and the stride is shown. Reception trace spans ±20 ms with 513 samples and derives from the same retarded-time solution. The reference's 600 Hz, 30 m/s, c=343 m/s approaching example is 657.508 Hz, not 705 Hz.

## FFT and actual microphone input

Generated teaching signals use fs=16384 Hz, N=2048, Δf=8 Hz, Nyquist=8192 Hz. Modes: sine, two weighted tones, and odd harmonics 1,3,5,7 weighted 1/h and normalized by the sum of weights. Only frequencies strictly below Nyquist are included; invalid frequencies are rejected. The generated modes do not play sound. Audio playback is not implemented.

The radix-2 FFT removes the finite-window mean, multiplies by the symmetric Hann w[n]=.5−.5cos(2πn/(N−1)), then computes one-sided peak amplitudes 2|X[k]|/Σw, with undoubled DC/Nyquist. Reported RMS is the original window RMS in digital full-scale units. Mean removal deliberately removes DC from the spectrum. Bin-local peaks use neighbors and threshold max(1e−5, .08×largest amplitude), top eight; no peak interpolation or claimed sub-bin resolution. Silence yields zero amplitude/RMS and no peaks. Leakage and bin width are explained in the UI; off-bin peaks can underestimate true amplitude. The waveform displays the first 256 actual samples and the spectrum all 1025 one-sided bins.

Microphone permission is requested only after selecting device input and pressing Start. `record` streams actual little-endian PCM16, requesting 48000 Hz mono, with enhancement controls disabled where supported. The native configuration-change callback supplies actual output rate/channels. PCM chunks handle odd-byte/interleaved-frame boundaries, average channels to mono and retain at most 4096 samples; FFT requires a complete 2048-sample window. Captured physical time accumulates new PCM frames divided by their actual output rate. Analysis runs in an isolate at most ten times per second with at most one job in flight, and stale jobs are discarded after stop or source changes. Configuration changes reset the window. Generated mode remains usable offline without permission; denial never substitutes generated data under a microphone label.

Listening has an explicit status and Stop. Navigation, section changes, background and disposal stop capture; resume never restarts it. Raw audio and waveform are never written or uploaded. Explicit Record/Save session can persist only the analyzed spectrum bins, derived values, source/rate/window and notes. Automatic session writes do not authorize a new microphone analysis snapshot; they may retain an already explicitly authorized snapshot. Reopen restores analysis paused, without waveform or microphone start. A microphone reading is relative full scale, **not dB SPL**; no calibration is claimed. Actual emulator PCM validation is separate from synthetic signal tests, and no physical acoustic fidelity is inferred from it.

## Geometrical optics

Angles are degrees from the surface normal in UI/storage; vector calculations use radians. Rays intersect the actual boundary plane before direction changes. Reflection uses r=d−2(d·n)n. Snell transmission uses homogeneous, isotropic indices n₁sinθ₁=n₂sinθ₂, vector refraction, critical asin(n₂/n₁) when n₁>n₂. Radicands within 1e−12 of zero clamp to zero; larger negative values are TIR and have no ordinary transmitted propagating ray. Speed c/n and wavelength λ₀/n change; frequency c/λ₀ stays constant. Rays show geometry only: there are no invented power percentages or Fresnel intensities.

Lens/mirror models use a real object u>0, positive focal length for converging lens/concave mirror and negative for diverging lens/convex mirror. 1/v=1/f−1/u; m=−v/u. Real v>0, virtual v<0; lens real images are right of the lens, mirror real images are in front (left). Object height is limited to .08 min(u,|f|). Lens principal rays use the thin-lens approximation. Mirror drawings use spherical radius 2|f| and local vector reflection; their small spherical aberration is not hidden, while the image marker is explicitly paraxial. Vertical image scale is visibly ×10; horizontal scene coordinates preserve their own metric mapping. A real image may lie outside the finite viewport and is labeled accordingly. Camera zoom/pan and numeric distances allow inspection without clipping a fake image at an edge.

At |u−f|≤1e−10 max(1,u), image distance/magnification/height are absent and image type is infinity. Primary recorded image vergence remains finite at zero. No huge finite substitute or NaN enters trials. Virtual extensions are dashed; physical screen exists only for real images, screen alignment error is actual distance error. Screen focus does not claim a diffraction spot calculation. Tests cover 3f,2f,1.5f,f,.5f and diverging modes.

## Optical interference and diffraction

Both are scalar monochromatic air/vacuum Fraunhofer models using sinθ=y/√(L²+y²), not a small-angle coordinate substitution. Double narrow slits: cos²(πd sinθ/λ), approximate spacing λL/d. No finite-slit envelope is silently added. Single uniform slit: (sinβ/β)², β=πa sinθ/λ; stable sinc limit at zero. Minima sinθ=mλ/a for nonzero integers |mλ/a|<1, exact positions L tan(asin(mλ/a)); central width is twice the first-minimum coordinate. Symmetric profiles use 801 samples over ±30 mm, and physical-screen brightness uses those identical computed samples. Detector readout evaluates the same analytic intensity. Moving only the detector reuses the cached profile.

L∈[.5,3] m; |θ|≤3.44°. Aperture/separation is dependently capped to sqrt(.1λL), so aperture²/(λL)≤.1, with no near-field solver implied. Wavelength 380–750 nm. The smallest permitted double-slit fringe has more than eight displayed samples. Slits/paths are enlarged instructional drawings; displayed RGB is illustrative and not a calibrated visible spectrum. No smoke, visible clear-air laser beam, or optical-frequency animation is claimed.

## Time, sampling and storage

Shared FixedClock advances at 120 Hz with existing bounded catch-up. Analytic state recomputes at 30 Hz, generated FFT at 10 Hz, history every eight ticks or explicit inspection; histories cap at 301 samples. On pause state is refreshed at the exact saved tick so replay/record agree. Temporal teaching factors: .1 for waves/pulses/coherent field, .001 standing waves, .0001 generated FFT, .5 Doppler. At maximum supported frequency these stay below temporal aliasing limits. UI speed .25…2× multiplies the declared teaching scale. Dynamic scrub/replay operates in physical time. Optics are static ray/intensity models with parameter/detector edits, rather than fake carrier-frequency Play. Reduced motion never autostarts; explicit Step/replay/plots remain available. No automatic simulation starts normally either.

Session configuration includes kind/model, SI parameters/units, integer ticks, mode, source, notes, guided work, prediction attempts and display preferences. DB schema stays 3; new quantities/models extend validated JSON support without rewriting old trial bodies. Incompatible sessions display a recovery notice. All earlier model identifiers remain supported. Typed trial values never store infinity/NaN. Completion needs a changed configuration, guided checks, a correct recorded prediction and an actual trial, not opening the screen.
