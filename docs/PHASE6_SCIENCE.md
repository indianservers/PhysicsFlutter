# Phase 6 scientific models and references

The executable specifications are generated in [PHASE6_LAB_SPECIFICATIONS.md](PHASE6_LAB_SPECIFICATIONS.md) from `etLessons` and `etControls`; they list every lab's equations, constants, units, conventions, supported control ranges, defaults, numerical methods and limitations. The engine is `lib/core/physics/electronics_thermal.dart`, model `electronics-thermal-6.0`. No Phase 7 model is enabled.

## Shared conventions and methods

All internal temperatures are kelvin, volumes m³, pressure Pa, energy J, entropy J/K, current A and resistance Ω. Celsius and Fahrenheit are conversions of a single underlying temperature. Gas heat Q is positive into the gas; work W positive by the gas; ΔU=Q−W. Cooling heatOut is positive into the ambient and ΔU+heatOut=0. Negative net radiative/convection heat rate denotes a colder body gaining heat.

Exact SI definitions: kB=1.380649e−23 J/K, NA=6.02214076e23 mol⁻¹, e=1.602176634e−19 C; R=kB NA. Stefan–Boltzmann constant 5.670374419e−8 W m⁻² K⁻⁴. Monatomic Cv=3R/2 and γ=5/3. See [NIST SI definitions](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2) and [NIST Boltzmann constant](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?k).

The existing 120 Hz `FixedClock` controls all playback, with bounded catch-up and pause on route/lifecycle changes. Thermal processes explicitly accelerate physical time by τ/5; amplifier playback uses 0.2/f physical seconds per displayed second. Scientific time is shown. Speed controls alter playback only. Scientific edits restart the run, except Boolean switch changes, which are timestamped within a bounded event history. Samples are bounded at 301, analytic profiles at 161, logic events at 200 and process-ledger states at 20. Replay uses initial inputs, mode, integer ticks and input events. No new database, units subsystem or independent wall clock was introduced.

## Electronics

Semiconductors solve n−p=ND−NA and np=ni² with a rationalized minority-carrier solution to avoid cancellation. ni(T)=1e16(T/300)^1.5 exp[−Eg/(2kB)(1/T−1/300)], Eg=1.12 eV. The 300 K anchor and simplified mobility law are declared teaching parameters, not calibrated device data. Bulk neutrality residual and mass-action product are exposed. Fully ionized/nondegenerate equilibrium is explicitly restricted; lattice dots and energy bands are schematic. Physical basis: [MIT 6.012 introduction to semiconductors](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-fall-2009/a37c73e7fcff4bcb5c5f1177e12d0891_MIT6_012F09_lec01.pdf) and [MIT law of mass action lecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-23-electrical-optical-and-magnetic-properties-of-materials-fall-2007/resources/clean13/).

Diodes solve the actual series operating point using a monotonic bracket between zero and the signed supply. Shockley current uses a small-argument expm1 expansion and a high-exponent sentinel only for bracketing, never a displayed infinite current. Bisection must satisfy a voltage residual before any reading is assigned. A failed iteration budget produces an explanation, never a guessed 0.7 V. Is(T) uses a declared constant-gap teaching law. The supply sweep, junction curve and resistor load line are calculated separately. The model excludes reverse breakdown, series junction resistance, capacitance and heating. Device-equation basis: [MIT 6.012 course lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-fall-2009/pages/lecture-notes/). Reference Is and ideality are adjustable model parameters, not claims for a named diode.

The generic NPN uses fixed VBE=0.65 V and a continuous piecewise collector characteristic with knee 0.15 V. Its exact intersection with the collector load line produces cutoff, active and saturation states; terminal-current conservation is exposed. No named transistor pinout, Early effect, temperature model, frequency response or realistic saturation-voltage claim is made. Amplifiers are ideal quasistatic closed-loop models, with resistor-set gain, actual sinusoidal input and symmetric output headroom clipping. They are not named finite-bandwidth devices. These deliberate approximations are described in Learn and Formulas rather than presented as measured component specifications.

All seven Boolean gates are evaluated directly. The active truth-table row and timing state share the same calculation; NOT ignores B. No propagation delay, fan-out or analog voltage is inferred. The IC is generic; its decorative leads are not a manufacturer pinout.

The TMP36 mode simulates the nominal transfer from the [Analog Devices datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/TMP35_36_37.pdf): .5 V offset, .01 V/°C, −40…125°C. It does not emulate accuracy/noise or access phone sensors. The LDR law is an explicitly illustrative power law, bounded at 1…10000 lx with saturation indication; a shared Phase 5 MNA divider solution determines voltage. Uniform resistor scaling preserves node voltages within the existing solver's resistor domain. It is not a precision lux calibration. Ultrasonic echo uses t=2d/c and the declared dry-air linear sound-speed approximation c=331.3+.606T°C over 0…40°C, distance .02…4 m. Outside this range means no echo, not an extrapolated distance. See [OpenStax sound speed](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/17-2-speed-of-sound).

## Thermodynamics

Temperature separates a sample reservoir/finite cooling sample from a negligible-capacity lagging probe. Both response equations are solved analytically, including equal sample/probe time constants. Finite-sample heat loss and internal-energy change balance exactly. Probe heat loading, spatial gradients and phase changes are excluded. The mockup's 58.4°C corresponds to 331.55 K, not an independent Kelvin input.

Conduction uses the steady constant-k linear rod solution and a spatial probe. Convection uses Newton cooling for a lumped body with a Biot-number gate ≤0.1 based on characteristic length m/(ρA). Radiation uses kelvin fourth powers, a large enclosure, view factor one and a gray surface. RK4 limits the local thermal stability step to at most .2 of the tangent time constant; its integrated heat-flux ledger balances the actual numerical internal-energy change. Convergence is checked by halving the step. Ordinary-temperature surfaces remain gray, without glow or unsupported smoke. Basis: [OpenStax temperature and heat](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-introduction), especially [heat-transfer mechanisms](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-6-mechanisms-of-heat-transfer).

Gas Laws has isothermal, isochoric and isobaric paths. First Law additionally includes reversible adiabatic paths. Only independent initial state and process targets are editable; P, final T, Q, W and ΔU are derived. Path duration is a visualization parameter, not a heat-transfer prediction. Work is analytic: nRT ln(V2/V1), zero, PΔV or nCv(T1−T2), respectively. The ledger exposes the conservation residual and saved process history. Basis: [OpenStax thermodynamic processes](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/3-4-thermodynamic-processes), [heat capacity](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/3-5-heat-capacities-of-an-ideal-gas) and [adiabatic processes](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/3-6-adiabatic-processes-for-an-ideal-gas).

Entropy has three distinct models. A reversible reference path evaluates ideal-gas state entropy. Free expansion is an endpoint comparison only, Q=W=ΔU=0, T constant and ΔS=nR ln(V2/V1); no irreversible quasistatic piston motion or identical-gas mixing entropy is claimed. Two finite constant-capacity bodies exchange heat in an isolated pair, approach the weighted equilibrium temperature, conserve energy and produce nonnegative total entropy. A body's individual entropy can decrease. Basis: [OpenStax entropy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/4-6-entropy) and [free expansion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/4-1-reversible-and-irreversible-processes).

The Carnot cycle has two actual isotherms and two adiabats. With r=V2/V1 and a=(Th/Tc)^(1/(γ−1)), V3=aV2 and V4=aV1. The P–V profile closes at the original pressure, volume and temperature. Stage/cumulative heat and work differ from reference full-cycle Qh, Qc and net work. The engine's efficiency is exactly 1−Tc/Th; 600 K / 300 K gives .5. Tests independently integrate the P–V area and check grid convergence, stage continuity, cycle closure and net heat/work. Basis: [OpenStax Carnot cycle](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/4-5-the-carnot-cycle).

These sources support the physical relationships. Adjustable teaching parameters and declared simplifications are project choices, not quotations or manufacturer specifications. The target boards supply visual guidance only.
