## Fluid Mechanics: s14-e00…05

1. **Density & Pressure:** ρobject=m/V, pabs=psurface+ρgh, pgauge=pabs−preference. Depth positive downward from a fixed surface; reference pressure changes the gauge zero only. The independent mass/volume specimen illustrates density; it does not secretly change the liquid density. Hydrostatic, incompressible, no capillarity or compressibility. Sources: [OpenStax density/pressure](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-1-fluids-density-and-pressure), [fluid equations](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-key-equations).
2. **Hydrostatics:** two cylindrical branches share connected base fluid; a lighter, immiscible upper layer of fixed volume may occupy the left. H=Voil/AL, Δh=(Δp+ρoil gH)/(ρg), zL=h0−ARΔh/(AL+AR), zR=h0+ALΔh/(AL+AR). Left top=zL+H. Thus AL zL+AR zR=(AL+AR)h0 and pressure at the common datum balances. The U passage below the datum adds a constant volume and is excluded from the reported *branch* volume; no fluid is created. Widths derive from circular branch area, heights from physical meters with the same scale. Reject inverted density ordering, empty/overflowing branches; no transient sloshing, mixing or meniscus pressure. Source: [OpenStax measuring pressure](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-2-measuring-pressure).
3. **Buoyancy:** exact rectangular volume Vsub=width×length×clamp(bottomDepth,0,height), Fb=ρfluid gVsub, W=ρobject gV. Static light body floats with submerged fraction ρobject/ρfluid; equal-density body has neutral fully immersed equilibrium; dense body rests on the tank bottom with explicit support force. Held mode reports required signed external support and free net upward force. A large overflow reservoir fixes the free surface; no displaced-level change, hydrodynamic settling or tilted body is simulated. The apparatus block dimensions and waterline share one world scale. Source: [OpenStax Archimedes](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-4-archimedes-principle-and-buoyancy).
4. **Continuity:** A=πr², v=Q/A. Cosine radius profile joins equal inlet/outlet radii through a variable middle section, including valid expansions. Passive markers advance in cumulative volume coordinates, not arbitrary canvas speed. A 2048-segment trapezoidal integral uses A√(1+z′²)dx so local speed along the centerline agrees with Q/A even in the elevated tube. Fixed 120 Hz playback, no numerical Navier–Stokes solution, turbulence or real fluid material claim. Pressure is not inferred in this lab. Source: [OpenStax fluid dynamics](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-5-fluid-dynamics).
5. **Bernoulli:** p+ρv²/2+ρgz=constant; p(x)=pin+ρ(vin²−v(x)²)/2−ρgz(x). Open piezometer head=(p−patm)/(ρg), top elevation=z+head, all drawn on the same physical scale as the pipe. Reject pressure at/below the chosen model vapour pressure and negative or overflowing open heads (0.14 m top capacity); never clamp a pressure into a plausible picture. Ideal steady inviscid flow; no loss coefficient, pump, cavitation simulation or temperature-dependent water property. Source: [OpenStax Bernoulli](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-6-bernoullis-equation).
6. **Viscosity:** m dv/dt=(ρsphere−ρfluid)Vg−6πμrv. τ=m/(6πμr), vt=2r²(ρsphere−ρfluid)g/(9μ); v=vt(1−exp(−t/τ)); y=vt[t−τ(1−exp(−t/τ))]. Stable expm1/small-time expansion avoids cancellation, and 80-step bisection finds the finite travel arrival. Bead stops at the ruler endpoint; no continuation through the vessel. Require denser bead, terminal Re=2ρfluid rvt/μ≤0.1, r/R≤0.02 for R=0.05 m, supported travel≤0.2 m. Main bead size is physical, inset explicitly ×12. Quasi-steady Stokes ignores added mass/history, wall/end corrections; wall effects can still be a few percent at the largest ratio. Source: [OpenStax viscosity](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-7-viscosity-and-turbulence).

## Modern Physics: s15-e00…05

1. **Quantum Ideas:** one-dimensional infinite well 0<x<L, ψn=√(2/L)sin(nπx/L), En=n²h²/(8mL²). |ψ|² is stationary; global phase creates no drifting probability. Separate ψ (m⁻¹/²), probability density (m⁻¹), normalized detection histogram and discrete eigenenergies. Seeded position measurements represent independent identically prepared systems, not the time path of one particle. Finite-grid sampling and finite histogram bins are approximations; mass controls En and does not alter an eigenstate's position distribution. Source: [OpenStax particle in a box](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/7-4-the-quantum-particle-in-a-box).
2. **Photoelectric Effect:** photon E=hf=hc/λ, Kmax=max(0,hf−φ), stopping potential Kmax/e. No emission below threshold, regardless of intensity. Explicit apparatus model photon flux=IA/(hf), emitted electron rate=QE×flux above threshold, saturated current=e×rate. Collection assumes a **uniform longitudinal kinetic-energy distribution** between 0 and Kmax: collection=clamp(1+eVbias/Kmax,0,1) for Kmax>0. At exactly zero excess energy, only positive bias collects. Zero intensity/QE produces zero current. Kmax=0 below threshold is a convention accompanying `emitting=0`, not evidence of emitted zero-energy electrons. No Richardson emission, material spectral response or actual current-noise measurement. Source: [OpenStax photoelectric effect](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-3-the-photoelectric-effect).
3. **Matter Waves:** nonrelativistic electrons λ=h/p, p=√(2me eVacc); E≤200 eV. Two identical coherent rectangular slits give unnormalized f(y)=sinc²(πay/(λD))cos²(πdy/(λD)), normalized **conditionally on the visible detector window**, not the whole beam. No claim of absolute flux or trajectories. Require a≤d/2, |y|max/D≤0.05, d²/(λD)≤0.01, and ≥4 histogram bins per fringe (160 bins). Fraunhofer, paraxial, perfectly coherent idealization; no wavepacket/which-path/decoherence model. Actual seeded y positions accumulate into the displayed histogram. Sources: [OpenStax wave–particle duality](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-6-wave-particle-duality), [double-slit diffraction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/4-3-double-slit-diffraction).
4. **Atomic Spectra:** hydrogen only, R_H=R∞/(1+me/mp), 1/λ=R_H(1/nlower²−1/nupper²), En=−hcR_H/n². Lyman/Balmer/Paschen emission and absorption reverse the same actual energy difference. Vacuum wavelength (Hα≈656.47 nm), distinct from the commonly quoted air value≈656.3 nm. Upper levels2…8 subject to upper>lower; finite line set and actual series limit. Absorption assumes atoms prepared in the chosen lower state, not an invented thermal population. UV<380 nm, visible380…780 nm, IR>780 nm. RGB and line brightness are illustrative, no transition strengths/fine structure/Lamb shift/Bohr trajectories. Source: [OpenStax Bohr model and hydrogen spectrum](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-2-the-bohr-model) (energy-level formula only).
5. **Nuclear Structure:** bounded neutral-isotope central masses listed below. Z+N=A, nuclear charge=Ze, B=[Zm(¹H)+Nmn−Mneutral]uc², B/A. Consistent atomic-mass convention approximately cancels electron masses; electron-binding corrections omitted. Neutral atom mass is labeled separately from nuclear charge, not called bare nuclear mass. The nucleon layout/camera is an illustrative composition diagram; it does not predict nucleon positions, shell structure, charge radii or stability. No generated isotope continuum, mass parabola, radioactive stability or half-life claims. Source: [OpenStax binding energy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/10-2-nuclear-binding-energy).
6. **Radioactivity:** λdecay=ln2/T½, Nexpect=N0 exp(−λt), A=λNexpect in Bq. Each fixed tick draws aggregate source losses Binomial(Nremaining,1−exp(−λΔt)), detected source counts Binomial(losses,η), background counts independently Poisson(bΔt). Counts, source decays, remaining atoms and expectation are separate quantities. No looping over 10⁹ atoms, no Gaussian population approximation. Presets **Cs-137: 30.05 y, Co-60 5.27 y**, rounded educational values from [PTB](https://www.ptb.de/cms/en/ptb/fachabteilungen/abt6/fb-63/information/background-information-in-the-area-of-brachytherapy.html). A year here is explicitly 365.25 days; no overclaim about evaluation precision. Source also [OpenStax radioactive decay](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/10-3-radioactive-decay). No daughters, branching spectra, dead time, shielding attenuation, dose or radiation-safety planning.

## Numerical methods, reproducibility and storage

Fixed 120 Hz model clock, capped work per displayed frame through existing FixedClock; long elapsed/background time is not integrated on resume. Playback rates change physical seconds per playback second, never physical equations. Finite run endpoints are exact tick limits (12 s playback for pipe/Stokes,20 s for the other evolving models). Stokes physical time is scaled to its calculated arrival; decay time is scaled to the selected number of half-lives. Replay recreates parameters, seed, streams and ticks. Input changes clear observations; camera azimuth is presentation only. Controls expose numeric input, sliders and apparatus drags, and unsupported combinations show a reason and disable recording.

Position PDFs use 4096 equal intervals, nonnegative trapezoidal normalization, binary-search inverse CDF with interpolation. Histogram reference bars use CDF differences per bin; observed bars use counts/(N×bin width). Histograms retain all counts (at most 2000 detections); last 300 raw positions are retained for the apparatus/record. Quantum analytic-CDF goodness-of-fit and matter-grid convergence are tested. Graph profiles are161 samples except discrete levels/isotopes. Pipe metric integral is 2048 intervals, tested against local speed and continuity.

PRNG xorshift32 with nonzero state; uniform=(state+0.5)/2³². Independent XOR-derived streams separate source, detector and background. Box–Muller normal, Marsaglia–Tsang gamma, beta-order-statistic recursive binomial (halve trials until≤32, then Bernoulli) and Knuth small-mean/PTRS large-mean Poisson. No approximate normal/Poisson replacement for binomial losses. Rejection loops have explicit guards; invalid sampling fails instead of inventing a reading. References: [Devroye, Non-Uniform Random Variate Generation, ch.X](https://numerical.recipes/devroye/Devroye_files/chapter_ten.pdf), [Devroye recursive-generation note](https://luc.devroye.org/devroye-recursivepoisson-1991.pdf), [Hörmann transformed-rejection Poisson](https://research.wu.ac.at/ws/portalfiles/portal/18953249/document.pdf). Floating arithmetic/finite PRNG impose numerical approximations; statistical tests are calibrated against theoretical standard errors, not exact count equality to expectation.

Trial records preserve model/dataset/sampler versions, SI input/state units, explicit profile-axis quantity/unit, derived profile units, histogram bounds/bin width/density units, mode, seed, ticks, current state, sampled histories, histogram counts and bin metadata (densities reconstruct from count/N/bin width), recent positions and observation type. Snapshot maps/lists are copied so later playback cannot mutate an in-memory recorded history. Sessions preserve notes, attempts, controls, speed and graph selection. Existing schema 3, all old model decoders and old IDs remain; Bq is a real activity quantity. CSV uses the existing complete JSON snapshot column plus primary SI quantity. No fabricated presets/results are inserted into the notebook. Completion requires a changed scientific configuration, matching correct prediction, actual recorded trial and notes. Opening a lab does not earn completion.
